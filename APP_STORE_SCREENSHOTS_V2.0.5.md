Screenshot captions and upload specifications for App Store Connect.

---

## Required Display Sizes

Apple requires screenshots for at least these two iPhone sizes (others inherit):

| Device Class | Resolution (portrait) | Example devices |
|---|---|---|
| **6.7" Super Retina XDR** *(required)* | 1290 × 2796 px | iPhone 15 Pro Max, 14 Pro Max, 13 Pro Max |
| **5.5" Retina HD** *(required)* | 1242 × 2208 px | iPhone 8 Plus |

Upload **10 screenshots max** per device class. Aim for 5–7 focused screens.

## How to Add Captions in App Store Connect

App Store Connect does not have a built-in caption overlay tool. Use one of these methods:

### Option A — Add text overlay before uploading (recommended)
Use **Canva**, **Figma**, or **ScreenshotCreator** to:
1. Place the device screenshot on the background.
2. Add the caption text at the top or bottom.
3. Export at the exact resolution listed above.

### Option B — Upload raw screenshots, add captions later
App Store Connect lets you drag screenshots and type a "display name" (internal only, not user-facing). Captions visible to users must be baked into the image itself.

---

## Upload Steps in App Store Connect

1. Open **v2.0.5** → click **Version Information**.
2. Scroll to **App Previews and Screenshots**.
3. Click **iPhone 6.7" Display** and drag/drop your 6.7" screenshots **in order** (Screen 1 first).
4. Repeat for **iPhone 5.5" Display**.
5. Switch localization to **Arabic** and upload the same screenshots — App Store Connect lets you reuse English screenshots for Arabic if the UI language is the same; or upload Arabic-captioned variants if you have them.
6. Save.

---

## Screenshot Design Tips

- Keep captions **short and bold** — 4–6 words, large font, high contrast.
- Use the **orange (#f97316)** accent from the app for caption backgrounds to stay on-brand.
- Show real listings with actual cars/parts photos — authentic content converts better than generic placeholder screens.
- For Arabic, ensure the caption text is **right-aligned** and uses a clear Arabic font (e.g. Cairo, Tajawal).
- Avoid placing important content in the top/bottom 10% — some device frames clip there.
