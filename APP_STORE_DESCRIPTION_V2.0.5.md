**Description** (4000 char limit — paste exactly):
```
Saman is the UAE's marketplace for cars and spare parts — buy, sell, and discover deals across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Al Ain and beyond.

🚗 BUY & SELL CARS
Browse thousands of new and used vehicles listed by owners and dealers. Filter by make, model, year, price, and emirate. Contact sellers instantly via in-app chat or phone.

🔧 SPARE PARTS & ACCESSORIES
Find the exact part you need — tyres, rims, engines, body parts, electrical components, and more. List your own parts in minutes and reach buyers across the UAE.

📍 UAE-WIDE COVERAGE
Listings from every emirate: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Al Ain, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, and Umm Al Quwain. Filter by your location to find what's near you.

💬 CONTACT SELLERS DIRECTLY
Message sellers in-app or call them directly. Save your favorite listings to revisit anytime.

📸 LIST IN MINUTES
Take photos, write a description, set your price — your listing goes live immediately. Manage, edit, or renew listings right from the app.

🔔 INSTANT NOTIFICATIONS
Get notified the moment someone messages you or a new listing matches your search. Never miss a deal.

🔒 VERIFIED & SECURE
Phone number verification on every account. Your privacy is protected — your number is never shared publicly.

Whether you're a private seller clearing out spare parts, a dealer listing vehicles, or a buyer hunting for a bargain — Saman is where UAE's automotive market meets.

Download free. No subscription needed to browse or buy.
```
