Yes. For your marketplace, SEO can become one of your strongest long-term acquisition channels, especially because every new car or spare-part ad can create another Google landing page.

The biggest opportunity I see is this: in my Google spot-checks, your homepage appeared, but I could not surface individual Saman listings for searches around Toyota, Nissan, BMW, etc. That suggests you may be leaving a huge amount of long-tail traffic on the table. Google specifically recommends unique, crawlable URLs, normal <a href> links, canonical URLs, sitemaps, and crawlable pagination for ecommerce-style sites.

I would attack it in this order:

Make every single listing its own Google-indexable webpage. A user posting a Patrol headlight should automatically create something like /spare-parts/nissan/patrol/y62/headlight-12345, with a Google title such as “Nissan Patrol Y62 Headlight 2020–2024 for Sale in Dubai | Saman Marketplace.” A vehicle could become “2022 Toyota Land Cruiser GXR for Sale in Dubai – AED 245,000 | Saman Marketplace.” The page should contain the price, emirate, condition, model/year, images, description and related listings in actual crawlable HTML. Google recommends descriptive URLs rather than IDs alone and consistent canonical URLs.
Build hundreds of SEO category pages automatically. This is where Saman could eventually become very powerful. For example: /spare-parts/dubai, /spare-parts/abu-dhabi, /spare-parts/nissan/patrol, /spare-parts/toyota/land-cruiser, /cars-for-sale/dubai, /cars-for-sale/nissan/patrol/dubai, etc. Do the same in Arabic. But only index combinations with real inventory; empty/filter/sort pages should generally not be indexed because huge numbers of filter URLs can waste Google's crawling.
Target UAE search terms rather than generic “automotive.” I would build dedicated pages around phrases such as car spare parts UAE, used car parts UAE, car parts Dubai, auto spare parts Dubai, used spare parts Dubai, cars for sale UAE, used cars Dubai, Nissan Patrol parts UAE, Toyota Land Cruiser spare parts UAE, Lexus parts Dubai, BMW spare parts UAE and Mercedes spare parts UAE. Arabic is equally important: قطع غيار سيارات الإمارات، قطع غيار دبي، قطع غيار مستعملة دبي، قطع غيار نيسان باترول، قطع غيار لاندكروزر، سيارات للبيع دبي، سيارات مستعملة الإمارات. Don't cram all of those into the homepage; make individual pages that genuinely satisfy each search.
Add structured data to listings. Spare-part pages should have Product information such as product name, image, price, condition and availability. Google specifically says Product structured data can make information eligible for richer Search, Images and Lens appearances, and it has a Product-snippet type for pages where users can't directly purchase online—which could be particularly relevant to a classifieds marketplace.
Fix the technical SEO foundation. Create an automatically updating XML sitemap containing every active listing and important category page, submit it to Google Search Console, give every indexable page a self-referencing canonical, ensure listings are linked using actual <a href> links, and give pagination crawlable URLs. Google explicitly notes that crawlers generally don't click JavaScript buttons to reveal more results, so infinite-scroll marketplaces need special attention.
Treat the App Store and Google Play as separate search engines. On iOS, Apple says search uses the app title, subtitle, keyword field, primary category and user behaviour including downloads, ratings and reviews. Apple now also lets you associate keywords with custom product pages. So I'd optimize Saman around terms such as Cars,Car Parts,Spare Parts,Used Cars,Automotive,UAE,Buy Cars,Sell Cars while avoiding duplication between the title/subtitle/keyword field. Apple gives the keyword field 100 characters. Google Play similarly uses the app name, descriptions, category and tags for discovery, and supports localized listings and store-listing experiments.
Then add off-site growth around the SEO. Get UAE garages, spare-parts shops, tuners, dealerships, automotive blogs and enthusiast sites to link directly to their Saman store/profile/listings. Create Instagram/TikTok/Snap videos around actual searches—“5 Patrol Y62 parts listed in Dubai today”, “Cheapest Land Cruisers listed this week”, “R8 parts currently for sale in UAE”—and link viewers to the corresponding SEO category rather than only the homepage. This creates a nice loop: sellers create inventory → inventory produces Google pages → Google/social sends buyers → more buyers attract more sellers.
What I would prioritize immediately

#1 by far: individual listings indexed on Google.

If you eventually have, say, 20,000 active listings, you potentially have 20,000 highly specific search landing pages instead of effectively relying on one homepage. You also gain all the category/model/emirate combinations above them. That is the marketplace SEO model I'd want Saman to follow.
